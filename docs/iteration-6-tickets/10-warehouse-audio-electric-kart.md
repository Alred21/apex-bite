## What to build

Upgrade the fixed zero-allocation Web Audio synthesizer with procedural indoor concrete-hall convolution reverb, multi-layered electric Kart powertrain and braking synthesis, modulated tire scrub, and hollow HDPE Barrier impact and graze acoustics, while preserving gesture-only activation and lifecycle silence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A procedural indoor concrete-hall impulse response powers a convolution reverb node built once during audio initialization, placing motor, tire, and Barrier sounds inside an enclosed arena space.
- [ ] Electric powertrain synthesis layers fundamental motor tone, inverter PWM switching harmonic whine, toothed belt mechanical texture, off-throttle regenerative braking pitch sweep, and hydraulic brake rotor hiss under braking.
- [ ] Tire and Barrier acoustics combine dual-band modulated rubber scrub and squeal with resonant hollow HDPE plastic Barrier impact thuds and continuous wall-rubbing texture scaled by inward normal impact speed.
- [ ] Updates, resets, mode switches, and Barrier contacts allocate zero audio nodes, audio remains off until an explicit user gesture, and tab blur, hidden visibility, open dialogs, Time Trial finish, and disposal silence playback cleanly.
- [ ] Browser audio verification confirms expected RMS output across volume changes, mute/pause, resets, Barrier impacts, and repeated disposal with zero console errors.
