## Parent

[Iteration 6 specification #50](https://github.com/Alred21/apex-bite/issues/50)

## What to build

Transform the surrounding warehouse shell into an authentic K1-style indoor karting arena—strictly outside the drivable Circuit corridor—featuring an overhead start/finish gantry with live start lights and scoreboard, a pit and staging bay with parked driverless Karts, a spectator mezzanine, structural hall architecture, and live LED corner marshal panels scaled cleanly across quality tiers.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An overhead start/finish steel truss gantry spans the straight without intruding into Chase Cam sightlines or the track corridor, carrying a live five-pod LED start-light tree and digital timing scoreboard synchronized to Time Trial state.
- [ ] A trackside pit and staging bay alongside the start/finish straight includes painted staging markings, a barrier gate, electric charging stanchions, and parked driverless static clones of the refined OTL Superleggero Kart.
- [ ] The enclosed industrial hall features exposed steel roof trusses, ventilation ducts, hanging high-bay LED fixtures, corrugated cladding walls, a spectator viewing mezzanine with safety glass, painted blue concrete infield with drainage grates, rubber tire stacks behind high-speed outer Barriers, and illuminated Apex Bite signage.
- [ ] Live LED corner marshal panels mounted outside key Turns display steady green during normal driving and flash yellow or red upon steep Barrier impacts or reverse driving.
- [ ] Performance quality omits purely decorative background props (such as parked pit Karts, ventilation ducts, and tire stacks) while retaining the track, seamless Barriers, start gantry, markings, and indoor lighting.
