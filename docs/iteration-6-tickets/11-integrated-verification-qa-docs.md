## What to build

Integrate all Iteration 6 realism pillars, verify end-to-end driving behavior and ≥ 60 FPS production browser performance with separated Ghost Karts visible across quality tiers, update all technical and licensing documentation, and deliver a complete evidence report.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Type checking, the entire automated unit test suite (including new kinematic, camera-comfort, and Barrier-miter checks), and the production build pass cleanly with zero regressions to `kart-v2` physics, Reference Lap, or saved training records.
- [ ] Automated Blender overlap audits and all browser verification harnesses (steering direction, Ghost Kart model parity, Ghost overlap suppression, Ghost steering replay, HUD layout, Kart audio, and presentation review) pass with zero page or console errors.
- [ ] Warmed production browser measurements in both Chase Cam and Helmet Cam with Full Racing Line, both separated Ghost Karts visible, and active audio sustain at least 60 FPS on Balanced and High quality, recording hardware, browser, viewport, DPR, draw calls, triangles, and frame-time percentiles.
- [ ] Final before-and-after showcase screenshots across quality tiers and views, updated graphics/presentation/audio/camera/license documentation, and an iteration implementation report are saved cleanly.
