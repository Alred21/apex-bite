## Problem Statement

After Iteration 5 introduced a Blender-authored OTL Superleggero silhouette and GRID Legends-style Barrier deflection physics, the driving experience still falls short of modern motorsport realism in four areas:

1. **Geometric, untextured Kart and driver surfaces**: The Kart and driver render from zero-UV vertex buffers, so physically based surface micro-detail (carbon weave, tire rubber grain, Nomex suit texture, painted clearcoat, and flush livery graphics) cannot map onto the meshes. Bodywork, bumper piping, instrument housing, equal-width tires, floating decal planes, and smooth mannequin limbs still look visibly synthetic up close.
2. **Incomplete physical movement**: Front steering tie rods stretch longitudinally rather than pivoting as rigid fixed-length bars in a real four-bar linkage. Kingpins lack caster and inclination angles, the rear drive belt and motor pinion lack synchronized motion, the brake pushrod and throttle linkage do not articulate with the pedals, driver boots stay frozen while pedals depress, and the driver's head and torso remain rigid during cornering.
3. **Partial overlap checks and overlapping Barriers**: Automated clearance checks only inspect three zones, leaving part-to-part seams, depressed pedals against boots, and articulated sleeves unchecked. Meanwhile, indoor Barriers are built from overlapping rectangular segments that protrude at corners and flicker along tight Turns such as Turn 3 and Turn 10.
4. **Barren indoor venue, flat lighting, bulky overlay, and dry acoustics**: The Circuit sits inside a plain warehouse box with uniform tiled pavement, disconnected oval skid stamps, generic room reflections, an oversized top-left control panel, and dry synthesized audio lacking indoor concrete-hall acoustics.

## Solution

Deliver a focused sixth iteration of the solo K1 Speed Toronto training simulator centered on visual, mechanical, environmental, camera, HUD, and audio realism—using GRID Legends and real-world OTL Superleggero indoor karting as the benchmark—while keeping the deterministic `kart-v2` physics solver, Circuit layout, Barrier collision corridor, and validated Reference Lap unchanged:

1. **Sculpted OTL Superleggero Kart and Articulated Driver with Real Physical Movement**: Upgrade to UV-unwrapped Blender-authored models and bundled local physically based textures. Give the Kart real-world hardware details (continuous wraparound molded Protex bumper with elastomer shock mounts, staggered wider rear slicks and narrower front slicks with dished split-rim hubs, beveled Apex Bite #07 crimson/white/carbon bodywork with flush livery and recessed fasteners, sleek integrated dash binnacle, and working rear LED brake/status beacon). Ensure every moving part obeys real physical kinematics: a single-piece rotating steering column and Pitman arm driving fixed-length rigid tie rods through tilted kingpin knuckles, synchronized rear live axle, ventilated brake rotor, sprocket, motor pinion, and belt teeth, connected brake pushrod and throttle linkage, visual-only suspensionless chassis vibration, tire sidewall flex, and bumper compression, plus a sculpted helmeted driver with two-bone inverse-kinematics arms locked to the steering wheel, pedal-tracking legs and boots, and subtle look-to-apex helmet and torso cues.
2. **Seamless Interlocking Protex Barriers, Rubbered-In Track, and K1-Style Indoor Arena**: Replace overlapping Barrier boxes with continuous spline-mitered molded Protex modules featuring vertical interlock pins, a flush continuous rubber rub-rail, and outer steel flex mounts. Upgrade the Circuit surface to sealed indoor concrete with a darker, glossier rubbered-in Racing Line groove, weathered grid markings, and continuous dual rear-tire skid ribbons. Surround the Circuit—strictly outside the drivable corridor—with an authentic indoor karting arena featuring an overhead start/finish steel truss gantry with live start lights and timing scoreboard, a pit and staging bay with charging stanchions and parked driverless Karts, a spectator mezzanine with safety glass, roof trusses, ventilation ducts, high-bay LED fixtures, blue painted infield concrete, tire stacks, Apex Bite signage, and live corner marshal LED panels.
3. **GRID Legends Lighting, Reflections, Clearcoat PBR, and Dynamic FX**: Replace the generic reflection environment with a custom high-contrast indoor-arena image-based lighting map featuring bright overhead LED light banks, apply clearcoat shading across bodywork, helmet, visor, and sealed concrete, ground every Kart with soft contact ambient occlusion under the chassis and all four tires across all quality tiers, enable selective high-dynamic-range bloom on emissive arena and Kart LEDs in High quality, and add restrained rear-tire scrub dust and Barrier-graze scuff particles.
4. **Immersion Camera, Sleek Motorsport HUD, and Indoor Warehouse Audio**: Add bounded look-to-apex yaw, speed-gated micro-vibration, optional default-off helmet visor periphery framing in Helmet Cam, and smooth dynamic yaw-lag in Chase Cam—strictly zeroed when camera motion is set to 0%. Restyle the HUD into a sleek, low-profile motorsport overlay with a toggleable Minimal driving mode. Upgrade the fixed zero-allocation Web Audio synthesizer with procedural indoor concrete-hall convolution reverb, electric inverter PWM whine, belt texture, regenerative braking sweep, brake rotor hiss, dual-band tire scrub, and hollow Protex Barrier acoustics.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Preserve Simulation Physics, Circuit, and Reference Lap**: The deterministic 120 Hz `kart-v2` solver, Barrier collision chords, shallow-graze Clean Lap tolerance, Circuit Centreline, Reference Lap, Corner Drills, and training record schema remain strictly unchanged. All realism upgrades reside in rendering, asset authoring, presentation-side camera presentation, HUD presentation, and Web Audio synthesis.
- **ADR-0006 Superseding Inline Zero-UV Meshes**: _Contradicts [ADR-0004](adr/0004-blender-authored-kart-and-driver-pipeline.md) (inline synchronous zero-UV mesh arrays) and the no-third-party-textures policy in earlier presentation docs, worth reopening because UV-unwrapped `.glb` assets with locally bundled CC0 and procedural PBR maps eliminate the 1.4 MB inline mesh bundle, enable genuine clearcoat/normal/roughness/livery detail, and preserve frame-zero Ghost Kart parity via startup preloading._ An asset preload module loads bundled local `.glb` models and PBR maps prior to renderer initialization, with a lightweight synchronous fallback rig when headless unit tests construct the Kart rig without browser assets. All external CC0 and original assets are documented locally with zero runtime network fetches.
- **True Physical Kinematics Module**: Shared deterministic solvers compute exact rigid four-bar steering linkage motion (one-piece rotating steering column, steering wheel, and welded Pitman arm driving fixed-length tie rods through tilted kingpin knuckles with caster, KPI, and Ackermann geometry—eliminating rod scaling) and analytical two-bone inverse kinematics for driver arms/wrists on steering grips and driver legs/ankles/boots tracking live brake and throttle pedals.
- **Sculpted Blender Pipeline and Automated Overlap Audit**: Local live Blender 5.2 (via MCP) and reproducible Python build scripts author the sculpted OTL Superleggero Kart, articulated driver, and modular indoor arena props, saving editable source files and UV-unwrapped `.glb` exports. An automated Blender BVH-tree overlap and connectivity audit validates static and dynamic clearance across full steering lock and pedal travel while keeping the Kart strictly inside its physical collision envelope and preserving the Helmet Cam yellow steering-wheel marker visibility.
- **Seamless Spline-Mitered Barriers and Rubbered-In Track**: A dedicated Barrier mesh builder generates continuous miter-joined molded Protex HDPE profiles (clamping acute inner hairpin miters with clean bevel splits), vertical interlock pins, continuous rubber rub-rail, and outer shock-mount brackets aligned strictly to the physical Barrier thickness. The Circuit track surface blends a rubbered-in Racing Line groove onto PBR sealed concrete, paired with a pre-allocated ring-buffer continuous dual rear-tire skid ribbon replacing disconnected oval stamps.
- **Indoor Arena Props and Live Trackside Feedback**: Modular arena props (start/finish gantry with live five-pod LED light tree and timing scoreboard, pit/staging bay with parked driverless Kart clones, spectator mezzanine, trusses, ventilation ducts, high-bay LEDs, tire stacks, and live LED corner marshal panels) sit strictly outside the drivable Circuit and scale across Performance, Balanced, and High quality tiers.
- **GRID Legends Lighting, Grounded AO, Selective Bloom, and Pooled FX**: A custom high-contrast indoor-arena cubemap replaces the generic room environment. Bodywork, helmet, visor, and track use physical clearcoat materials; grounded contact-AO quads sit under the chassis and all four steered tires in every tier; High quality enables selective HDR bloom strictly above threshold 1.0 on emissive LEDs; and a zero-allocation pooled particle system emits restrained scrub dust and graze scuffs in Balanced and High while keeping Ghost Karts isolated from shadows, particles, and bloom bleed.
- **Bounded Immersion Cameras, Sleek HUD, and Warehouse Web Audio**: Camera presentation adds bounded look-to-apex yaw, speed-gated micro-vibration, optional default-off visor periphery framing, and smooth Chase Cam yaw-lag—all strictly zeroed at 0% motion strength. The HUD restyles existing panels into a sleeker motorsport overlay keeping all control IDs accessible in Full mode plus a `[H]` Minimal toggle. Web Audio adds a startup-baked indoor concrete-hall convolution reverb impulse response, layered electric powertrain/belt/regen/brake synthesis, and hollow HDPE Barrier acoustics with zero per-frame allocations.

## Testing Decisions

- **Approved Test Seams**:
  1. **Public Simulation Session Seam (primary)**: Exercise driving inputs, snapshots, Reference Lap replay, camera comfort bounds, and portable training-data import/export through the existing public session API, ensuring zero regression to `kart-v2` physics, timing, Clean Lap rules, or `0%` camera motion invariants, and covering new bounded look-to-apex and Chase Cam yaw-lag bounds.
  2. **Production Browser QA Harnesses (extended only for async asset readiness)**: Exercise the real WebGL renderer, HUD, and Web Audio graph via existing browser harnesses (steering direction, Ghost Kart model parity, Ghost overlap suppression, Ghost steering replay, HUD layout and `[H]` Minimal mode, Kart audio lifecycle, presentation review, and full separated-Ghost 60 FPS integration performance across Balanced and High). Good browser checks assert observable canvas pixels, bounding layout, audio RMS, diagnostics draw/triangle budgets, zero page errors, and frame-time distributions rather than internal scene-graph structure.
  3. **Offline 3D Geometry, Kinematic & Miter Audit Seam (focused offline checks)**: Automated Blender BVH-tree overlap checks across steering/pedal extremes, pure unit tests for fixed-length rigid four-bar steering and two-bone arm/leg IK math, and pure Barrier miter mesh integrity assertions (no inverted/degenerate faces and strict corridor clearance).
- **Prior Art**: Follows the existing deterministic session tests, camera-comfort bounds tests, real-canvas steering/ghost/HUD/audio browser verification scripts, and the Iteration 5 Kart proportion and clearance suite.

## Out of Scope

Changes to `kart-v2` physics, Circuit layout, Barrier collision geometry, or the validated Reference Lap; paid 3D/texture assets or runtime remote asset downloads; third-party commercial venue trademarks; opponent AI racing, multiplayer, career progression, or driver avatar customization; and production server deployment.
