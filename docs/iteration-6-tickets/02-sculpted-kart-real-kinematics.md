## What to build

Upgrade the Kart to a sculpted, UV-mapped OTL Superleggero with real-world hardware details and authentic physical mechanical movement across steering, wheels, rear powertrain, footwell pedals, rear brake lighting, and suspensionless micro-dynamics, automatically mirrored on both Ghost Kart types without altering `kart-v2` physics or Circuit dimensions.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Kart features a continuous wraparound molded Protex bumper with elastomer shock mounts, staggered wider rear slicks and narrower front slicks on dished split-rim aluminum hubs with locknuts and valve stems, beveled Apex Bite #07 crimson/white/carbon bodywork with flush UV-mapped livery, recessed fasteners, battery covers, routed high-voltage cables, and a sleek integrated digital dash binnacle.
- [ ] A working rear LED brake and status beacon on the roll hoop housing illuminates brightly under braking and pulses amber when driving in reverse.
- [ ] Steering motion is physically continuous: the F1 steering yoke, full steering column, U-joint, and Pitman arm rotate together as one assembly, driving constant fixed-length rigid tie rods through spherical Heim joints to tilted kingpin knuckles with realistic caster, kingpin inclination, and Ackermann angles without any longitudinal rod scaling.
- [ ] The solid rear live axle, cross-slotted brake rotor, driven axle sprocket, spinning motor output pinion, and toothed drive belt animate synchronously with wheel speed.
- [ ] The brake pedal drives a pushrod coaxially into the hydraulic master cylinder and the throttle pedal articulates its linkage into the throttle sensor box.
- [ ] Restrained visual-only chassis vibration, tire sidewall flex, and slight Protex bumper compression on Barrier contact enhance physical presence without feeding back into `kart-v2` physics, timing, or training records.
- [ ] Automated overlap and kinematic checks verify zero part-to-part interpenetration, constant tie-rod length, full steering-lock wheel clearance, retention within the physical collision envelope, and unobstructed visibility of the Helmet Cam yellow steering-wheel marker.
- [ ] Both Reference Lap and Personal Best Ghost Karts inherit the sculpted Kart and real steering/wheel kinematics automatically while remaining isolated from shadow casting and overlap interference.
