# Preserve K1 Toronto training fidelity

Apex Bite remains a simulator for learning the actual K1 Toronto Circuit, preserving its 11-Turn layout and realistic electric Kart speeds. We address keyboard controllability through progressive, speed-sensitive inputs and improved grip behavior before reassessing Circuit scale, rather than increasing dimensions and speed together. Keyboard Assistance translates driver inputs while leaving Racing Line choice and braking decisions to the driver, so accessibility preserves the training goal.
